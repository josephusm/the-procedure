Current known errata:
No unresolved release-blocking gameplay defect is recorded in the public issue surface as of 2026-07-23.

Known correction-surface limitation:
GitHub issue filing may require a GitHub account or active session. Blank issues remain enabled and keep observations from being forced into a category before the first sentence, but they do not create a no-account intake route. If that boundary blocks a release-relevant defect, accessibility failure, trace discrepancy, or cold-run pressure mark, the boundary itself belongs in pre-1.0.0 review.

This is a current trace, not a promise. New defects, accessibility failures, public trace discrepancies, source/artifact mismatches, reuse-boundary failures, warmed doorway regressions, source/provenance mismatches, recovery failures, account-gated intake limitations, or cold-run playtest pressure filed before 1.0.0 can still interrupt release review.
